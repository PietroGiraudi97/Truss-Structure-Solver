Getting started
  1. Pick a template (top bar), or draw: Node tool → click to place joints; Member → click joint-to-joint.
  2. Click a joint with Support to pin it (cycle pin/rollers).
  3. Load: click a joint, enter Px/Py in the panel.
  4. Press ▶ Solve (or S). Colors show stress; green = deflected shape.
  5. Shortcuts: 1–5 tools, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+S save, F fit.

Help & shortcuts

Workflow

  1. Draw — pick a template (top bar) or use the Node tool to place joints, Member to connect them.
  2. Support — click a joint to cycle pin / rollers. You need at least 3 reaction components.
  3. Load — click a joint and enter Px / Py in the panel.
  4. Material & section — set E, A, density and σ_allow for new members (Step 2). To change a single member, select it with the Edit tool and edit its properties in the floating box.
  5. Solve — press ▶ Solve (or S).
  6. Learn — use the Step 5 tools to see the method of joints, sections, and influence lines.

Units

Geometry in m, loads in kN, E in kN/cm², A in cm², displacements in mm, stress in MPa.

Stability

A stable planar truss needs m + r ≥ 2j and at least 3 non-concurrent reaction components. The status bar shows the counts.
